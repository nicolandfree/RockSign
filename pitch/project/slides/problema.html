The problem

Freelancers close deals by word of mouth. And words get lost.

Scope creep

“While you're at it, could you also…?” With no written scope, every extra ask looks included.

Late payments

Without signed dates and amounts, getting paid depends on the client's memory.

“I never said that”

When a dispute comes up, the only evidence is what each side remembers from the call.

Writing a contract after every call takes hours. So it gets postponed… and never happens.

Three pains every freelancer recognizes. The agreement exists: it was said on the call. What's missing is someone writing it down and getting it signed. If you have your own data (surveys, freelancer interviews), put a number here.
