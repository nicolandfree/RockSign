Why now

Three curves crossed in the last few years.

Calls became data

A minute of audio costs under a cent to transcribe by machine, $1.99 by a human. AI reads the terms out of it.

Dollars on Solana

About $8.1B of USDC is on Solana, more than 10% of all USDC. A transfer costs about $0.0005.

Workers want stablecoins

57% of independent workers would accept them; 18% in emerging markets already get paid in them.

Sources: OpenAI and Rev pricing (2026) · Solana Compass (Aug 2026) and Solana docs · Stripe (Aug 2026, n = 2,300, 20 countries).
