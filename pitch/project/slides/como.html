How it works

From the call to USDC, without writing a line.

01

Record

Record the call or upload it, in English or Spanish. Whisper transcribes it in the browser.

02

Extract

Claude pulls out scope, dates, fee and payment plan. Each clause links to the second it was said.

03

Sign & seal

Both sides sign the hash. The client needs no wallet and no SOL. Sealed on Solana.

04

Get paid

USDC deposit on signing, balance on delivery. One tap or Solana Pay QR. No gas for the client.

Anyone can verify the document against the chain. Signed agreements export to PDF with a QR to the seal transaction.

This is the real flow in the live app. Step 03: the hash and both signatures go into a Solana memo that RockSign pays for. Step 04: the balance unlocks when the freelancer signs a delivery notice. The freelancer also sees what they received in pesos at the live best bid. Ask for recording consent at the start of every call.
