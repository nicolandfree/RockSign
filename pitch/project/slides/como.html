How it works

From call to signature, without writing a line.

01

Listen

Joins your Meet, Zoom or Teams call and tells everyone it's recording.

02

Understand

Detects deliverables, dates, amounts, revision rounds and payment terms.

03

Draft

Writes a clear draft, with every clause linked to its minute in the call.

04

Sign

When the call ends, it goes to every participant. They sign from their phone.

Optional review: the freelancer can tweak the draft before it goes out.

Four steps. Step 03 is the differentiator: every clause is tied to the exact moment in the recording where it was agreed. The recording notice in step 01 is not optional: it's what makes the agreement defensible and earns the client's trust.
