Roadmap

From the first signed agreement to a network of trust.

MVP

PHASE 2 · [Q_ 2027]

Live

Bot for Zoom, Meet and Teams. Templates by trade: design, dev, video.

Follow-through

Delivery and payment reminders tied to the agreement. Invoicing integrations.

Phase 1 can be built in weeks: it needs no live bot, just processing a recording. It validates whether the generated draft is good enough for the client to sign without changes.
